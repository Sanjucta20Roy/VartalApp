# Privacy Policy for ColdText Keyboard

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Developer:** Sanjucta Roy (`Sanjucta20Roy`)  
**Application:** ColdText Keyboard (`com.sanjucta.coldtext`)  

---

### Overview
Your privacy is extremely important to us. **ColdText Keyboard** is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ColdText custom keyboard application.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Processing
* **No Keystroke Logging:** ColdText **does not** record, collect, track, or log any keystrokes, phrases, typed text, or personal information.
* **On-Device Storage Only:** All custom phrases, keyboard size preferences, tone selections, and theme settings are stored locally on your device using standard Android `SharedPreferences`.
* **No Cloud Transmission:** ColdText does **not** transmit any text, phrases, or user data to external servers or third-party cloud services.
* **Smart Suggestions:** Contextual phrase suggestions are generated entirely on-device by analyzing text in the active input field using standard Android `InputConnection` APIs.

---

### 2. Permissions & System Access
* **`BIND_INPUT_METHOD`:** This standard Android system permission allows ColdText to function as an on-screen keyboard service. It enables inserting selected phrases into text fields when you tap phrase keys.
* **Vibration Permission:** Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback (keypress vibration) on button taps.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
ColdText does **not** integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
ColdText does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this repository.
